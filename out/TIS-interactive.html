Defaults to TIS-retained tonnes — the fixed-price tonnes sold to clients. Partner principal is repaid at par (= landed cost), so ICE cancels on partner tonnes; only TIS’s fixed-price tonnes carry ICE risk. Raise to full cargo only if partner repayment is fixed-VALUE rather than par/landed-cost.

FX Hedge (Naira Exposure)
FX Hedge ON — unconfirmed values are marked INDICATIVE. Verify all before live trading.
Enable FX Hedge in Deal tab to activate.

Fee and spread are a small fraction of every USD of notional — typically 0.001–0.004 ($1–$4 per $1,000 hedged), not whole dollars. 0.004 on a $19M hedge ≈ $76k cost. Entering whole-dollar figures (e.g. 2.0 = $2 per $1) overstates the cost by ~1000×.

NDF cost drivers: the bank posts initial margin % of notional (financed at the credit rate over tenor days) plus a flat broker fee. Margin is bank-provided, never partner equity.
